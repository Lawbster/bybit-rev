## Attribution and decision criterion

Full baseline versus waiting economics is authoritative. For first-deep-timer
cohorts, compare whole completed episode outcomes matched by original first
entry timestamp. Report both baseline W/L dollars and matched waiting delta;
removed baseline episodes are NOT zero-dollar alternatives. Replacement
episodes/open marks are separately reconciled through full attribution.
One ladder's delta cannot be counted as the marginal profit of one rung.

Do not select a condition solely because cheaper fills are frequent. Look for
better entry-price balance without disproportionate missed upside, favorable
matched-episode contribution in both TP assumptions and earlier/recent views,
adequate independent episode counts, and no concentration in one event/month.
No condition passes net/monthly/DD qualification without its own subsequent
frozen full-occupancy replay. Existing blanket waiting still retains its known
older-window, execution-model and DD failures. Conditional arms are next,
not tuned and run after inspecting these labels in this same job.
