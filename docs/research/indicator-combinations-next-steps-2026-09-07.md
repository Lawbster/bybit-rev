## 1. Direction

Continue **standalone entry selection first**, then explicitly separate timing
effects, then selected HL/S/R conditions, and finally actual ladder decisions.
Do not couple a new indicator condition with new exits, sizing and calendar
filters in the same experiment.

C01 tested eight entry triggers against four single state conditions. It did
not exhaust combinations: RSI/ROC/Bollinger/ATR entry families were absent,
and improving momentum, volatility shocks, waiting for confirmation and
calendar eligibility were not tested. The justified conclusion is that the
32 exact C01 pairs failed their screens, not that combinations cannot help.

More coverage is worthwhile, but “exhaustive” cannot mean every mathematical
product of indicators, thresholds, clocks and exits. Track coverage by a named
mechanism and a capped card. More backtests on already-mined history are not
more independent evidence.

## 3. T01: time gating means entry eligibility, not selected TP hours

First perform a descriptive breakdown of **all entry opportunities**, executed
trades and occupied/rejected crossings. Use fixed UTC blocks 00-04, 04-08,
08-12, 12-16, 16-20, 20-24, plus a separate weekday/weekend table. No adaptive
session boundaries, country-market labels or cross-product of hour and weekday.

Report counts, original winning/losing dollars, exposure and calendar coverage
beside each subgroup. Trade outcomes can be grouped by the historical signal
time descriptively; they cannot be used to choose that trade in its own replay.
Group exit PnL separately so a TP that occurs at18:00 does not imply entering
at18:00 is beneficial.

The [TP atlas](../../research/codex-astra-tp-hl-event-atlas-findings-2026-09-07.md)
contains known winning closes under changing live configurations. It can raise
questions but cannot select profitable entry hours on its own. Repaired historical
bar timing also does not prove exact old collector publication times.

**Proposed subsequent replay, if this stage is approved:** use the unchanged
MFI15m dip parent (441/106 full/recent closes) and CMF30m continuation parent
(613/157), chosen now for denser opportunity sets, not after seeing an hourly
PnL leaderboard. For each parent test excluding exactly one of the six fixed
UTC blocks: **12 definitions**, no combinations of exclusions. Weekday/weekend
stays descriptive unless separately approved as new definitions.

Evaluate eligibility using the original signal timestamp. A signal outside
allowed time is discarded, never queued until the next block. A +1m fill can
cross a block boundary without relabeling the original decision. Exits and
protection remain unrestricted; the 12h hold starts at actual fill. Add a
clock-boundary regression and re-simulate occupancy from scratch.

Do not start calendar optimization on C01-30's 29 trades; splitting it into
six bins leaves very little evidence. Four-hour indicator signals also alias
to fixed hours, so do not call missing hourly opportunities an edge. Calendar
filters need same original/readiness/exposure comparisons and full monthly
budgets as other filters. In-sample favorable hours are not a fresh holdout.

## 4. S01: waiting for price recovery is a different strategy

This is not simply “use an indicator at a later time.” It adds a durable
research pending-signal state and changes when exposure begins.

Proposed four unfiltered long parents: original 15m CRSI into<=5, P2 RSI dip,
P4 MFI dip and P3 hourly ROC dip. Two deadlines per parent: **15 or60 minutes**.
Eight sequence definitions; preserve each parent's own original 12h exit.

At a fresh trigger while flat:

1. Freeze the high of the latest fully completed 15m bar available at the
   trigger. This common reference may precede a5m RSI trigger; record its exact
   source interval. Do not substitute a future15m bar or the trigger's forming bar.
2. Starting strictly after the trigger, enter at the first completed15m close
   **above that frozen high**, provided its close time is within the deadline.
   Confirmation at the exact deadline is considered before expiration. This
   uses an observed recovery, not the highest/lowest future price in a window.
3. Until confirmation/expiry, consume new A crossings without replacing or
   extending the pending signal. Missing required coverage invalidates the
   pending signal; no retrospective gap repair can resurrect a live decision.
4. On confirmation, freeze evidence and apply the case's normal fill delay.
   No confirmation means cancel, not enter at the deadline. Start the 12h
   hold from actual entry; exits remain unchanged.

Controls: immediate parent plus **fixed15m and60m waiting entries without the
price condition**, each using the same pending/occupied rules. Those eight
fixed-wait rules are **new control definitions**, not free repetitions.
Therefore S01's budget is16 new definitions /64 window-delay cases, plus
repeated parents, clocks and explicitly enumerated readiness controls.

The fixed-delay controls help distinguish waiting from selective confirmation;
they do not perfectly match exposure or each actual confirmation time. Keep
that limitation visible. Availability-only controls follow the same pending
observation schedule, entering at the first valid post-trigger closed15m
observation within the deadline with the price predicate removed; they are
not the fixed-deadline waiting controls. Enumerate these logical controls
separately and deduplicate only after exact equality is demonstrated. Full simulation,
not matching only the eventual confirmed parent trades, determines the net.

No simultaneous price cap, stop optimization, trailing entry, two-bar vote,
rearm extension or best-price selection. Any such idea is another definition.

## 5. Retained leads and bounded refinement

C01-30 stays the strongest incremental lead, but more threshold mining does
not create independent trades. C01-20 stays a small positive comparison. A
larger raw profit from a parent is not proof that a filter improved it.

After C02 and/or the timing review, select at most four mechanisms for a
separate R01 card, at most three changes each (12 total). Test one axis per
variant: neighboring level, one alternate context clock, or a separately
specified sequence. Never change all three together. Retain the original
parent, old pair and failures. A sample-limited setup may remain parked instead.

If nothing qualifies, report that. A budget-failing but mechanism-specific
idea can be proposed for exploratory work only under an explicit new card;
do not relabel it as a screen pass or silently weaken the old thresholds.

## 6. Acceptance and reporting remain fixed

Retain C01's research criteria unless a future card explicitly changes the
objective **before** running. Any changed screen must be labeled separately;
old C01 conclusions are never rescored into passes.

- At least30 full /10 recent closes in each delay, positive net and extra-cost
  net, solvent, valid timing/accounting, and no monthly marked regression
  worse than-$320 versus either original or readiness parent.
- Profit: at least+$500 full /+$200 recent in each delay; DD no worse.
- Defense: retain90% of positive parent net, reduce losing dollars, reduce
  full DD by20% and at least1 percentage point, no recent DD worsening, and
  beat the ex-post exposure-scaled parent on DD and losing dollars.
- Keep the inherited strict clock screen separate. Neither passing a research
  screen nor avoiding one known crash constitutes live authorization.

Always show baseline and variant together: W/L counts, winning/losing dollars,
open mark, net, fees/stress, DD/MAE, monthlies, concentration and newly enabled
paths. Return all attempted IDs, not just winners. Counts from repeated windows,
delays, controls and independent checkers are not independent market samples.

Use chronological/monthly robustness panels without renaming already-seen data
as untouched out-of-sample. A later train/test study must freeze selection on
earlier data and exclude training trades whose holding periods overlap the
test boundary. Historical indicators can warm up causally from earlier bars;
future outcomes cannot select earlier rules. Genuine future observations remain
separate, with a prospective cutoff fixed before collecting their outcomes.

## 7. Only then: HL/S/R and real ladder performance

For selected mechanisms add one known-time S/R or quality-verified HL condition
at a time, on identical healthy overlap with indicator-only/readiness and
relevant pulse/SR controls. Do not treat candle CMF/MFI/OBV as exchange aggressor
flow, or pre-TP observations as conditions that were necessarily known at entry.

Finally map a candidate to one ladder decision: initial entry, genuine deep
price-drop add, last-rung size, or exit. Reproduce the exact current canonical
ladder baseline on the same window before variants. Attribute extra/foregone
TP cycles and avoided/new forced closes across every month. Standalone dollars
cannot be added to ladder PnL or used to unpause the existing short owner.
