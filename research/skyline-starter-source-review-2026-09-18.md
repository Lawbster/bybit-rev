## Source rules and suggestions

- **Liquidity location:** equal highs/lows, recent trend extrema, daily/weekly extrema and range boundaries; references lie beyond candle wicks, not bodies (pp6–8). Claims that banks cause the subsequent move are the author's interpretation, not observed order identities.
- **Sweep entry illustration:** sell-side sweep, then first impulsive up candle, then long toward buy-side liquidity; additional confluence is required (p9). The asserted immediate opposing sweep is not an established outcome guarantee.
- **Orderblock boundaries:** entire last down candle before a strong up impulse for bullish OB; entire last up candle before a strong down impulse for bearish OB (pp11–12). Retest entry is described; bullish stop at the zone bottom, bearish stop at the top, with liquidity targets (pp11–13). This is a full-candle source definition, not proof that all other authors use identical zones.
- **Quality factors:** strong-volume impulse, liquidity cleared, BOS, adjacent FVG, remaining directional liquidity, freshness and HTF significance (p14). Page15 explicitly says the block need not meet every factor. Do not convert this list into an all-required Boolean gate. HTF-to-LTF refinement is recommended (pp4,14).
- **Targets and management:** staged partial profits at successive liquidity levels (pp4,9); put targets before the next opposing OB rather than beyond it (p15). A slow reaction warrants discretionary reassessment and possible closing, not a specified automatic timeout (p15).
- **Risk suggestions:** at most 2% account risk and at least 1.5:1 reward/risk are the author's recommendations (p4), not adopted settings or empirical optima. Partial-target weights are absent.

## Evidence and specification limits

The guide supplies illustrations and teaching hypotheses, not an audited trade denominator. Its 95%-loss statistic, strategy-superiority claims and explanations of bank intent are unsupported within the supplied document (p3). Neither examples nor promotional language validate profitability.

An executable specification would still need impulse/volume thresholds, equal-extreme tolerance, causal swing confirmation, precise entry/fill rules, stop buffers, target priority and partial ratios, timeframe mapping, freshness limits and response timeout. These are unspecified, not permission to invent source rules. Keep this independent author's OB/sweep guidance beside—not merged into—the RektProof sequences.
