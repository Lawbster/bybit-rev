# Existing knowledge: retrieval routes, not new strategies

These routes preserve what predates the PDFs. They are deliberately not
hundreds of copied parameter cards. Follow the exact study/card in the index
and [tested register](../TESTED-SETUPS.md) before claiming anything is untested.
Results are asset/window/implementation-specific; controls and caveats remain
in the authoritative findings. No new results were computed for this library.

## KN01 — Oscillator exhaustion and recovery

**Coverage:** RSI, CRSI, ROC, Bollinger dip/reclaim; individual indicators,
extreme thresholds and later combinations. Start at INDICATOR-FINDINGS and
I02/I04/I05/I07 in TESTED-SETUPS, then the exact family card.
**Lesson:** different extremes, timeframes and crossing/recovery conditions are
different entries. Failed tested cells do not prove an indicator universally
useless; a narrow positive result does not justify all thresholds.
**Reuse:** closed-bar indicator tape/formula tests and standalone execution.
**Before extension:** freeze entry crossing versus level occupancy, repeated
signals, structural or fixed stop/target, expiry and execution lag. Don't make
an “oversold winner” filter by inspecting the later recovery.

## KN02 — Momentum, trend strength and efficiency

**Coverage:** MACD, ADX/DMI, ATR-normalized movement, efficiency; I06/I08/I09.
**Lesson:** direction, strength and volatility are different measurements.
ATR by itself has no direction. Indicator entry tests do not validate a new
ATR stop or protected-swing regime classifier.
**Reuse:** MACD/ATR-efficiency findings, indicator tape and math validation.
**Before extension:** define continuation/reversal expectation and stop/target;
attach modifier to unchanged parent opportunities before making portfolio claims.
The PDF's qualitative “impulse” should not silently inherit whichever historic
ROC threshold won an unrelated study.

## KN03 — Participation and volume-derived indicators

**Coverage:** VWAP/RVOL, OBV/MFI/CMF; I10/I11 and later context combinations.
**Lesson:** signed candle-volume proxies are not observed aggressor orderflow;
CMF is not actual funds moving into/out of an account. Session VWAP, rolling
VWAP, anchored VWAP and volume-at-price profile levels remain different objects.
**Reuse:** existing indicator formulas and source-specific volume context.
**Before extension:** asset/venue and volume units, reset/anchor availability,
lookback completeness and entry clock. Do not replace missing measured flow
with a similarly named indicator without declaring a new mechanism.

## KN04 — Indicator combinations and time gating

**Coverage:** C01/C02/T01/T02/R01/H01, retained combination register and
context-filter replays. **Lesson:** exclusions can change which later trades
are occupied; improvement is not necessarily prediction at the removed entry.
Distinguish profit-only screen from the complete qualification screen.
**Reuse:** existing frozen combinations, retained producers and occupancy
attribution. **Before extension:** one defined interaction, unfiltered parent,
each component alone, actual replacement trades and own-window monthly deltas.
Don't use one selected clock boundary as general advice for all assets/sessions.

## KN05 — Calendar/rolling levels, rejection and reclaim

**Coverage:** LV01–LV03, HT01–HT03 and historical S/R studies.
**Lesson:** previous-calendar-day high is not rolling 48h high. Touch, wick
rejection, close-back and later confirmation have different information/fills.
Simple rejection did not reproduce every PA01/PA02 ancestral sequence.
**Reuse:** saved maps/signals, level-playbook methods and independent audits.
**Before extension:** freeze level age, availability, first/repeated visit,
event order, stop/target and entry eligibility. Do not rebuild the historical
map for every threshold or select levels from today's surviving map.

## KN06 — Auction/profile level behavior

**Coverage:** volume POC/NPOC daily/weekly/monthly maps, PB01/PB02, PH01/PH02,
PI01/DB01, LV/LC controls. **Lesson:** descriptive winner/loser context is not
earnings from a filtered replay; timed and bracketed exits need their own
baseline. Volume POC is not TPO POC; touch entry is not old-range return targeting.
**Reuse:** cached causal profile map, event samples and source-quality flags.
**Before extension:** profile type/venue, naked status at signal time, market
coverage, entry versus target role and occupancy. This library does not resume
or change the active POC checkpoint.

## KN07 — Measured orderflow and liquidity-pressure entries

**Coverage:** historical short signal ledger, HL bid-pull/taker/book composites,
OI/funding/liquidations and selling-acceleration versus price-response work.
**Lesson:** source-arrival clocks and available historical coverage matter.
Read later short revalidation/forward evidence, not only original attractive
backtests. A book imbalance, aggressive selling and falling price are distinct
conditions; spoofing/intent is not directly established.
**Reuse:** exact frozen policy, collectors' schema/availability and short/flow
research. **Before extension:** side, entry/exit, stale inputs, venue transfer,
duplicate signals, max concurrency and risk. Literature liquidity proxies
must not be treated as equivalent to observed HL flows.

## KN08 — Cross-asset and relative-price context

**Coverage:** BH01 BTC moves / BTC–HYPE return gaps, related waiting-entry
studies, RP02 descriptive atlas and BTC/SOL context comparisons.
**Lesson:** descriptive following returns are not a strategy replay; asymmetric
data gaps can bias a comparison. Raw return gap is not beta-neutral residual
or an executable hedged spread. No universal asset-transfer claim.
**Reuse:** causal joins, threshold events and known BTC coverage audits.
**Before extension:** leader/follower lag, common complete tape, original
opportunity baseline, delay, target/stop and replacement ownership. CTX01's
protected-swing cross-asset state is adjacent, not already proven here.

## KN09 — Ladder construction and exposure allocation

**Coverage:** component audit, L02–L09, FR01, AG10-E1 and related construction
studies. **Lesson:** timer adds, genuine price-drop adds, half sizing and add
vetoes change different risks. A final-rung reduction can reduce loss size
while sacrificing winners; show both. Context arriving at full exposure can
be too late to help construction.
**Reuse:** exact current-parent causal ladder replay and attribution. **Before
extension:** parent profile/version, add reason, inventory/risk ceiling,
rebuild after partials, gate persistence and full exit stack. Old pre-repair
or B17 figures cannot be carried into Agg10 claims without new own-parent parity.

## KN10 — Targets, partial exits and loss mitigation

**Coverage:** LV02 bracket grids, short exit mitigation, SRP01 pulse/map partial
audit, F02–F06, L11–L15 and later S/R branches.
**Lesson:** realized partial proceeds are not added strategy earnings. Stops,
timeouts, soft-stale TP and resistance pricing can change future re-entry and
winning runs. “Lower average loss” alone does not establish better total net.
**Reuse:** exact position-allocation/execution models and unchanged-parent
controls. **Before extension:** reason priority, full/partial quantities,
residual stop/TP, fill assumptions, time anchors, fees/funding and cutoff
inventory. EXIT01/EXIT02 are new source concepts, not certified replacements.

## KN11 — Regime permission, cooldown and recovery participation

**Coverage:** damaged-regime latch, short regime revalidation, Agg10 exits/
cooldown, SRR01 and waiting-entry interactions.
**Lesson:** a context label, a policy blocking entry and an exit are separate
actions. A warning must reach the intended inventory before it can help;
false blocks can miss recoveries. Time spent out of the market changes occupancy.
**Reuse:** exact policy chronology, warning/reach ledgers and release semantics.
**Before extension:** new ladder versus adds versus existing exits, latch memory,
cooldown origin, re-arm and release, input failure behavior and counters.
Asset-neutral library knowledge is not permission to alter live trend gates.
