## Comparison contract

Full: **2025-07-01 00:00 to 2026-09-04 19:01 UTC**.
Recent: **2026-05-17 20:43 to the same cutoff**, starting flat and overlapping.
Each independent rule uses $10,000 entry notional, $32,000 reference equity,
one position and a 12h exit from actual fill. Both entry and exit receive the
case's delay. Modeled fees are 0.055% each side; extra-cost stress adds 5bps
each side. **Before funding; not incremental live-ladder or account PnL.**

Research CRSI uses the validated prior-return rank, not the rounded legacy
live formula. OBV is normalized signed-volume change, not raw cumulative OBV.
The B observation is its expected last completed bar at the fresh A crossing.
No delayed confirmation or time-of-day trading filter was tested in C01.
