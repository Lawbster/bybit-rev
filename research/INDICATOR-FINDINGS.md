### I05 ROC expansion: bounded dip entries, momentum and one fragile short

**Validated:** unrounded `100*(closed price / close N bars ago - 1)`, N+1
contiguous closes; N=1/5/12 on 5m/15m/30m/1h/4h. ROC is a percentage return,

while current price is flat or still falling; ROC-zero exit is not break-even.
